IronLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

IronLog is a free workout tracker. It is built to work offline-first: your data lives on your device unless you choose to create an account.

What we collect

If you create an account: your email address, name (optional), and the workout data you log (sessions, sets, weights, personal records). Nothing else — no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs.

Where it lives

Local mode: everything stays on your device. Cloud sync: workout data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) protected by row-level security, so only your authenticated account can read your rows.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account & Data removes your workout data from the cloud and this device immediately. You can also email us to request deletion.
